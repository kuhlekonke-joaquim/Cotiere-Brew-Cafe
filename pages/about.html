About Côtière Brew Café

Welcome to Côtière Brew Café, a welcoming café based in Gqeberha, South Africa. Established in 2023, the café was founded by two coffee lovers with a passion for fresh coffee, delicious food and creating a comfortable environment for customers.

Côtière Brew Café aims to bring together quality food, refreshing drinks and friendly customer service. Whether customers are stopping by for a quick coffee or spending time with friends and family, the café provides a space where everyone can enjoy fresh coffee and fresh moments.

OUR STORY

A Café Built Around Fresh Moments

Côtière Brew Café was established in 2023 in Gqeberha, South Africa. It was created by two coffee lovers who wanted to offer customers a welcoming place to enjoy freshly prepared coffee and food.

The café focuses on creating an enjoyable experience for a variety of customers, including students, professionals, families, tourists and coffee lovers.

WHAT WE STAND FOR

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide fresh coffee, quality food and friendly customer service while creating a comfortable and welcoming environment for everyone.

Our Vision

Our vision is to create a café experience where customers can enjoy great coffee, delicious food and meaningful moments in a welcoming environment.

A PLACE FOR EVERYONE

Made for Coffee Lovers & More

Côtière Brew Café is designed to welcome a wide range of customers. Students can enjoy a comfortable place to spend time, professionals can stop by for a coffee, and families and friends can meet and enjoy food together.

Tourists and coffee lovers can also discover what the café has to offer through its selection of food and drinks.
